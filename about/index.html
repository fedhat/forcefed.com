About

Force F.E.D. is a one-person media and software shop.

The company

Force F.E.D. LLC is an independent creative organization specializing in engineering, entertainment, experimentation, and other activities as assigned. We produce videos for YouTube, build and run websites, and develop mobile apps. Revenue comes from advertising, affiliate partnerships, and app sales.

The company was formed as a media company to publish the Fed Learns To Make YouTube channel and has since grown into web and app development. It is based in Los Angeles, California.

The person

Force F.E.D. is run by Federico Hatoum, a software developer, maker, and YouTuber. Born in Argentina and based in Los Angeles, Fed writes software for a living and makes things for fun. His workshop projects, including woodworking, 3D printing, electronics repair, and the occasional tool review, end up on the company's YouTube channel.

In a previous life he made short films and appeared in a handful of commercials, which is where the company's taste for a good bit comes from.

The name

"Fed" is Federico's nickname, and Force F.E.D. began as a joke among college friends: an entirely fictional elite unit with an official-sounding acronym. The joke stuck, the acronym evolved, and today F.E.D. stands for Freakin' Engineering Department. The badge is held up by the department's only employee.
