I Love Caulk

The honest, plain-English guide to sealing things properly. Room-by-room guides go joint by joint through kitchens, bathrooms, windows, basements, roofs, and exteriors, explaining what goes in each gap and why. A Caulk Finder asks six questions and names the right formulation and a tube to buy.

Because learning sticks better with a high score, the site also has Bead Master, a game about laying a clean bead, plus a caulk crossword, a trivia quiz, and a tube calculator. Editorial picks are independent; Amazon links on the reviews page are affiliate links.

Visit ilovecaulk.com

Invincible Space Dinosaur

A math arcade game for kids. Pick a mission (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, squares, cubes, or primes) and a difficulty, then shoot the attackers that match each wave's target number. Misses become review cards and short drills, and progress unlocks ranks, badges, and new looks for your dinosaur in the Dino Lab.

Built for grown-ups' peace of mind: a one-time purchase with no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking, and no network requests at all. Several kids can share one device, each with their own grade, dinosaur, and scores.

Visit invinciblespacedinosaur.com
