Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy covers the website at forcefed.com, operated by Force F.E.D. LLC ("we", "us"). Our apps and other websites have their own privacy policies, linked from each product.

What we collect

Nothing on purpose. This website has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not set cookies.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub may log your IP address and standard request data for security and operations, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Pages also load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. Google's handling of those requests is described in Google's privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply and for our own records. We do not share or sell them, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Links

We link to other websites, including YouTube and our other projects. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

This website is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to contact@forcefed.com.
